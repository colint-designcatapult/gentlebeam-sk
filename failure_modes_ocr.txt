1: Purpose
The purpose of this Software Modes and Effects Analysis (SFMEA) is to evaluate the potential failure modes in the Internal and External GUI
Software, understand their effects on the system, patient, and other users, prioritize risks, and recommend corrective actions to mitigate those
risks. The purpose is to ensure the safety and effectiveness of the device throughout its lifecycle.
2: Scope
The scope of this SFMEA covers the Internal and External GUI Software, derived from its design. This document to be continuously maintained
and updated throughout the lifecycle of the medical device, as potential software failure modes are addressed and mitigated to achieve an
acceptable or As Low As Possible level of risk.
3: Abbreviations and Definitions
Abbreviaton/Termn
ALAP. As Low As Possible
Software Design Document
SFMEA Software Failure Modes and Effects Analysis
4: Overview
The Internal GUI console provides a means for the user to maintain demographic patient data, including treatment plans, and perform imaging. It
communicates with the record and verify component, flange camera stream, imaging component and robotic arm controller.
The internal GUI software runs on the All-In-One console located on the base of the system.
The external GUI console provides a means for the user to activate and monitor the treatment. It communicates with the main control board,
treatment head camera and imaging head camera.
The external GUI software runs on the external tablet console located outside the room.

8: SFMEA Risk Acceptability
The following Risk Zones define the 3 levels of Risk Acceptability:
Risk Evaluation Actions Method
Reduce risks within this region to a Zone 2 or Zone 1 risk with control measures or
design improvements. If none of these are feasible, document that the risk has been
reduced as far as possible and evaluate whether the benefit outweighs the residual risk
or eliminate the feature or design completely.
Reduce all Zone 2 risks with risk control measure(s). If the Zone 2 risk directly results
ALAP from design and/or manufacturing failures, pursue additional controls, or design
improvements to reduce the risk. If none of these are feasible, document that the risk
Zone 2 has been reduced as low as possible and evaluate whether the benefit outweighs the
residual risk.
Within this region, the level of risk is considered low and considered to be acceptable
once the risk has been reduced as far as possible. Reduction of risk to as far as
possible can be demonstrated through conformance to requirements or specification
and/or implementation of risk control measure in the risk management documents.
